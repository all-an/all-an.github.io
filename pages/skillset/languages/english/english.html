English Conjugation

A timed drill for English verb forms. Conjugate as many as you can before the clock runs out.

How to play:
  • You get 60 seconds. Each verb shows a base form and the tense to produce.
  • Type the conjugation and press Enter.
  • Right answers score points and build a streak; the streak adds a bonus.
