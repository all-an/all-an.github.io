DataSource & pooling

Boot auto-configures a DataSource backed by HikariCP, a fast connection pool, from your datasource properties. You can tune pool size and timeouts in configuration.

spring:
  datasource:
    url: jdbc:postgresql://localhost:5432/store
    username: app
    password: secret
    hikari:
      maximum-pool-size: 10
      connection-timeout: 30000  # milliseconds
