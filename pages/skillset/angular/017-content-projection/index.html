Content projection

<ng-content> projects markup supplied by a parent into a component’s template — the basis of reusable wrappers like cards and dialogs. Multiple slots use select.

<!-- card.component template -->
<div class="card">
  <header><ng-content select="[title]"></ng-content></header>
  <div class="body"><ng-content></ng-content></div>
</div>

<!-- usage -->
<app-card>
  <h2 title>Report</h2>
  <p>Projected body content.</p>
</app-card>
