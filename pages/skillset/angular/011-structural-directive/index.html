Structural directive

Structural directives (prefixed with *) add or remove DOM subtrees. *ngIf, *ngFor and *ngSwitch are the classic trio.

<!-- Conditionally render and repeat elements -->
<p *ngIf="isLoggedIn">Welcome back!</p>

<ul>
  <li *ngFor="let item of items; index as i">
    {{ i + 1 }}. {{ item.name }}
  </li>
</ul>
