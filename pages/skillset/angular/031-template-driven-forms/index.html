Template-driven forms

Template-driven forms are declared in the template with ngModel and directives. They suit simple forms; Angular builds the form model implicitly.

<!-- Requires FormsModule -->
<form #f="ngForm" (ngSubmit)="submit(f.value)">
  <input name="email" ngModel required />
  <input name="password" type="password" ngModel />
  <button [disabled]="f.invalid">Log in</button>
</form>
