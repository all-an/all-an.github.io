Async pipe

The async pipe subscribes to an Observable or Promise in the template, renders its latest value, and unsubscribes automatically when the component is destroyed.

<!-- No manual subscribe/unsubscribe needed -->
<ul>
  <li *ngFor="let user of users$ | async">{{ user.name }}</li>
</ul>

<p>Now: {{ clock$ | async | date:'mediumTime' }}</p>
