Decimals & place value

Arithmetic

The idea

Decimals extend base-10 positional notation to negative powers of 10 to the right of the decimal point: tenths (10⁻¹), hundredths (10⁻²), thousandths (10⁻³), and so on.

Key properties & formulas

Simon Stevin (1585) popularized decimal fractions in Europe. A reduced rational p/q has a terminating decimal expansion if and only if q's prime factors are only 2 and 5; otherwise it repeats periodically.
