Exponents & powers

Arithmetic

The idea

Exponentiation aⁿ repeats multiplication n times: a is the base and n is the exponent.

Key properties & formulas

Power laws turn multiplication of same-base powers into addition of exponents, leading naturally to a⁰ = 1 (for a ≠ 0) and negative exponents a⁻ⁿ = 1/aⁿ.

aᵐ × aⁿ = aᵐ⁺ⁿ (aᵐ)ⁿ = aᵐⁿ a⁰ = 1 a⁻ⁿ = 1 / aⁿ

Historical & theoretical context: Repeated multiplication. Core concepts include base, exponent, power laws.
