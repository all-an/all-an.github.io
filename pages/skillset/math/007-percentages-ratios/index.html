Percentages & ratios

Arithmetic

The idea

A ratio a : b compares two quantities multiplicatively; a percentage expresses a ratio as parts per hundred (per centum, /100).

Key properties & formulas

A proportion a/b = c/d holds if and only if the cross-products match: ad = bc. Percentage change measures relative growth or shrinkage from a baseline.

p% of x = (p / 100) × x % change = ((new − old) / old) × 100

Historical & theoretical context: Per centum · cross-multiplication. Core concepts include proportion, rate, scaling.
