Fractions & rational numbers

Arithmetic

The idea

A fraction p/q represents p parts of a whole divided into q equal parts. The set of all ratios of integers (with q ≠ 0) forms the field of rational numbers ℚ.

Key properties & formulas

Two fractions a/b and c/d are equivalent when ad = bc. Dividing numerator and denominator by gcd(p, q) reduces a fraction to its unique lowest terms.

a/b + c/d = (ad + bc) / (bd) (a/b) × (c/d) = (ac) / (bd)

Historical & theoretical context: Field of rationals ℚ. Core concepts include numerator, denominator, equivalence.
