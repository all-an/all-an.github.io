Arithmetic

The idea

The nth root ⁿ√x is the inverse of raising to the nth power: (ⁿ√x)ⁿ = x, or in fractional exponent form x^(1/n).

Key properties & formulas

The Pythagoreans discovered that √2 cannot be written as any ratio of integers p/q—the first known irrational number.

ⁿ√x = x^(1/n) √(a × b) = √a × √b
