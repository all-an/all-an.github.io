Order of operations

Arithmetic

The idea

Operator precedence conventions (PEMDAS / BODMAS) ensure every arithmetic expression has a single unambiguous value.

Key properties & formulas

Parentheses evaluate innermost first, followed by exponents, then multiplication/division left-to-right, and finally addition/subtraction left-to-right.

2 + 3 × 4² = 2 + 3 × 16 = 2 + 48 = 50 ≠ (2 + 3 × 4)²

Historical & theoretical context: Operator precedence convention. Core concepts include PEMDAS, precedence, grouping.
