GCD & LCM

Arithmetic

The idea

The greatest common divisor gcd(a, b) is the largest integer dividing both a and b; the least common multiple lcm(a, b) is the smallest positive integer divisible by both.

Key properties & formulas

From Euclid's Elements Book VII: gcd(a, b) = gcd(b, a mod b), and for positive integers a and b, their product satisfies gcd(a, b) × lcm(a, b) = a × b.

gcd(a, b) = gcd(b, a mod b) gcd(a, b) × lcm(a, b) = a × b

Historical & theoretical context: Euclid's Elements (Book VII). Core concepts include common divisor, common multiple.
