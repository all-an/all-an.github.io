Modular arithmetic

Number Theory

The idea

Modular arithmetic is a central topic in Number Theory built around congruence, clock arithmetic, residues. Its development is rooted in Gauss, Disquisitiones (1801), providing both theoretical structure and practical computational tools across mathematics and computer science.

Key properties & formulas

Key principles in Modular arithmetic connect congruence, clock arithmetic, residues. Mastering these relationships makes it possible to analyze structures rigorously, transform expressions into canonical form, and implement reliable algorithms in code.

a ≡ b (mod m) ⇔ m | (a − b) gcd(a, m) = 1 ⇒ a^φ(m) ≡ 1 (mod m)
